Current sponsored projects

Three ongoing collaborations with the City of Austin.

Housing Opportunity Index

We are updating Austin’s Housing Opportunity Index to guide affordable housing investments. Combining spatial analysis with community perspectives, the project examines access to everyday destinations and identifies neighborhood needs that public policy can address.

With Elizabeth Mueller · City of Austin Housing Department

Explore the project ↗

Displacement early warning system

We are developing a predictive model to identify areas at risk of renter displacement before it occurs. The project evaluates frequently updated local data to help City staff recognize emerging pressures and act earlier to support residents.

With Elizabeth Mueller · City of Austin Housing Department

Weatherization and indoor heat

We are studying whether weatherization and energy efficiency upgrades reduce indoor heat exposure and improve comfort in low-income single-family homes in Austin’s Eastern Crescent. The study combines indoor sensor measurements, electricity use, and resident surveys to inform heat resilience programs.

With Kevin Lanza · In partnership with Austin Energy
